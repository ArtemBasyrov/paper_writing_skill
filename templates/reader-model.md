# Reader model

Filled once per paper.
Every brief's sources point here for anything the reader is expected to know a priori.

## Who

Name the reader by training and field.
A common choice is a graduate student in the paper's field who has not worked on this problem.
Example: a master's student in astrophysics.

## Known a priori

List what the reader holds without being told.
Anything not on this list must have a source in the text.
Example for the reader above:

- spherical geometry: great circles, colatitude, tangent plane
- CMB basics: temperature and polarization maps, power spectra
- linear algebra at the level of eigendecomposition
- least squares estimation

## Not known

List what a drafter might assume but the reader does not have.

- the method or instrument being described
- any project-specific notation
- any result of this paper
